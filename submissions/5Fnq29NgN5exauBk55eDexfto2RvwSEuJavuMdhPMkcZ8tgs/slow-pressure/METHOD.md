# Slow pressure: let the model close its thinking itself, and commit where it had to be pushed

This method stacks on the published edit named in `entry.json` (`parent`), called the start below. It is about the
answers whose thinking never ends: they reach the token limit and score nothing. Six hardly used MLP units are
rewritten (`set_rows` on `gate_proj` and `up_proj`, `set_cols` on `down_proj`): two at layer 6, four at the last
layer.

- Computed in the run, from the start's own answers to practice problems: the reads (a ridge regression, two
  discriminant directions, a constant direction), every threshold, the write directions (linear solves through the
  final norm and the output head), and which units are rewritten.
- Fixed by the method: the layer of the first pair (the reason is the layer table of section 1), the sizes of the
  three pushes and the constants of the gates (reasons in section 2).

The run has two stages, because the last-layer units are fitted on answers generated by the model that already
carries the first pair. The figures of sections 1 and 7 are the author's measurements on practice problems with
the published edit that the parent named in `entry.json` is itself stacked on (the parent's own layer acts on
instruction requests and leaves these answers as they are); section 6 adds a run of these scripts on a local
build of the parent's method. The steps say what to measure first and when to stop.

## 1. The behaviour to change, and why it is there

**An answer that reaches the token limit scores nothing, and most such answers never closed their thinking.** On
that edit, 19% of the mathematics practice answers are cut at the limit: 13% with the thinking
still open, the rest in an answer part that turned into more reasoning. The other practice sources hardly reach
the limit.

**Cutting the thinking by hand does not help.** With the thinking closed by hand at a fixed depth and the same model
left to go on (mathematics, answers still thinking at that depth, split by what they did when left alone):

| closed by hand at | never-closing answers: right, answer left to the model / opened with `\boxed{` | answers that would have closed later and been right: the same |
|---|---|---|
| 12,000 tokens | 1% / 24% | 41% / 75% |
| 20,000 tokens | 3% / 21% | 44% / 77% |
| 28,000 tokens | 7% / 26% | 43% / 100% |

After a close by hand the model goes on reasoning in the answer part and reaches the limit again. Made to open
with the box it is right in a fifth to a quarter of the cases, and the answers that would have finished by
themselves lose a quarter of theirs. A hard cut needs to know which answers will never
close, and a length read cannot tell: at 20,000 tokens four in ten of the answers still thinking will finish,
almost half of them right.

**A pressure that rises slowly makes the model close by itself.** The first pair of this method (section 2) adds,
at layer 6, a push towards the closing tag that grows over several thousand tokens once a length read passes a
threshold. On 800 mathematics practice problems that no fit of the run had seen (2 samples each, paired by
problem; the screen of that run chose the budget 0.16):

| | before | first pair alone | both stages |
|---|---|---|---|
| thinking never closes | 13.2% | 0.1% | 0.1% |
| cut at the limit | 19.2% | 4.8% | 1.0% |
| finished without a result | 0.9% | 6.6% | 0.9% |
| right | 63.5% | 65.6% (+2.1 +- 1.0) | 66.6% (+3.1 +- 1.1) |

With the budget 0.12 the same two builds gain +1.9 +- 1.0 and +2.7 +- 1.0 (cut at the limit 8.4% and 3.1%).
On the hardest level of these problems the right answers go from 29% to 34% with both stages.

Under the pressure a good part of the answers close while it is still small: of the closes the pair takes part
in, about four in ten happen while it lets through less than a fifth of its write. There the push only tips a decision
the model was already close to, at the end of a line, and what follows is an answer like any other.

**Where the pressure enters decides whether it works.** The same pair at the same budget (0.12), with its read
fitted at the layer it sits in, on dev problems (mathematics, 2 samples):

| layer of the pair | answers cut at the limit (before: 18.0%) | mean tokens per answer (before: 14,700) |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | 7.8% | 14,800 |
| 8 | 25.0% | 16,300 |
| 10 | 13.3% | 15,000 |
| last | 18.8% | 15,300 |

Deeper in the network the pair still closes the thinking, but the close is one the model did not prepare: the
answer part runs on to the limit as after a cut by hand. Entering early, the push passes through the rest of the
network and the model brings its line to an end first.

**What stays wrong is what follows a close that needed the full pressure.** Those answers are not finished. The
model keeps reasoning inside the answer part, the pressure closes it again, and only the text after the LAST
closing tag counts as the answer: one line without the boxed result, or more reasoning until the limit. With the first pair
alone, late answers with one closing tag are right 57% of the time, with two 27%, with three or more
13%; over a quarter of all mathematics answers have several tags.

**Pushing only once would be worse.** Continued by the model without the pair from just before their second tag,
with no pressure any more, the answers with several tags are right 15% of the time (20% as generated) and
more than half of them run to the limit: the repeated closes are what ends them.

**So the result has to come at once after a pushed close.** The second stage raises the opening of a boxed answer
right after a closing tag that the pressure pushed, and nowhere else. Fitted on the model without the pair, at
closes inserted by hand where the gate would be half open, such a pair reaches 91% of those and about half of the real closes in
generation: the real ones come earlier in the rise and with the first pair's write in their context. Fitted on
answers generated by the model that carries the first pair, it brings the answers cut at the limit and those
without a result to the figures of the table above. Where the model closed by itself the commit must stay silent:
restarting the model's own answers with the box cost 2.8 points in mathematics (9 right answers lost, 2 won of
250).

**Suspected cause.** The model closes its thinking at the end of a line that states a result it accepts. How long
the thinking has run takes no part in that decision, although the length is marked in its state (the clock of
section 2 reads it). Where the thinking passes near such a line, a small push is enough. Where it is far from one,
a close leaves the model with an answer part that it treats as more thinking.

## 2. The units

Each part is a pair of units with the same reads; the second has a shifted gate and the opposite write, so that
the pair's write stops growing (a flat top). A unit computes `down * silu(gate . x) * (up . x)` from its layer's
MLP input `x`; `m` is a read with `m . x ~ 1` (the MLP has no bias).

**The clock (`derive/clock.py`).** A ridge regression of log(tokens generated so far) on the MLP input of layer 6.
Position by position it is noisy (error 0.28 in log units). The first position at which a whole answer crosses a
threshold is regular: `clock.py screen` measures, on problems held out from the fit, how many never-closing
answers cross a threshold in time, how many right late answers and how many early-closing ones do, for a series
of thresholds, and chooses one (the budget: the share of the self-closing answers that reach the threshold
before their own close).

**Exit (`derive/exit.py`, stage 1, layer 6).** Gate: one smooth step,
`sharp * (clock + lam * thinking - offset)`, where `thinking` is the discriminant read of thinking against answer
positions, centred on its median in late thinking. Up: the constant read. Write: at that layer, the vector that at
the final residual raises the closing tag and holds the tokens that end the turn or open a thinking block (a
frequency-weighted least-squares solve through the final norm and the head). Fixed constants:

- `lam = 0.25`: the thinking read lowers the gate in answers without adding much of its own noise to the clock.
- relative change of the layer's gate matrix 0.30 (the limit of an edit is 0.5 per matrix): this sets `sharp`.
  The ridge direction has a large norm, so the step is wide: from open to saturated the length grows by about
  half. That width is what lets the model choose its line.
- push 60 logits where the step has saturated: measured with a hook before any unit was written, of a push meant
  to be 40 logits 27 to 30 arrive at the output, and pushes under 20 arrive at a third.
- flat top at 2 gate units, up scale 4: numerical choices that keep both units' rows of similar size. Below the
  threshold the pair's write is slightly negative (at most 8% of the full push): shortly before the threshold it
  leans against a close.

**Break and commit (`derive/commit.py`, stage 2, last layer).** A closing tag counts as pushed when the exit pair
let through at least a fifth of its full write somewhere in the 32 positions before it, as the model's own when the
pair let through less than a fiftieth since the previous tag. Break: at a closing tag, raises the `\n\n` token (12
logits; after its own closes the model writes it anyway, after a pushed one about half of the time). Commit: at
that `\n\n` after a pushed tag and at the backslash after it, where the prompt contains `\boxed`, raises the
backslash and `boxed` (14 logits: the model's own log-probability of the backslash there is about -5). Both are
fitted on the stage-1 model's own answers, read through that model; in answers whose first close was pushed, the
tag is also inserted at up to two earlier positions where the pair let through half of its write, to add
positives. The other positions collected are the negatives: the two positions after the model's own closes,
thinking positions, the answers the model wrote by itself, every backslash, blank line and brace in them. The
threshold of each pair leaves out the 0.1% of them that look most like its own positions, and the builder reports
where the pair still fires.
The writes are exact logit changes through the final norm and the head, which is why these two pairs sit in the
last layer: a last-layer unit changes the output of its own position only, so on the fixed stage-1 model what it
does is known before any generation. They act at three positions after a pushed close, and step F measures what
they cost elsewhere.

**Which units.** In each layer, among the units whose largest write at the sampled trace positions is in the
lowest tenth, those with the smallest mean write; never a unit that the start's own chain of edits wrote.

## 3. Setup (first 15 min)

The bundle holds the start in `parent/edit/`. On the agent machine, from the unpacked bundle (the brief says to
copy `task.json` to the sandbox):

```
scp -F ~/.axonry/ssh/config task.json sandbox:/workspace/work/
scp -F ~/.axonry/ssh/config -r submission/derive sandbox:/workspace/work/
scp -F ~/.axonry/ssh/config -r parent sandbox:/workspace/work/        # gives /workspace/work/parent/edit/edit.json
```

In the sandbox (`ssh -F ~/.axonry/ssh/config sandbox`):

```
cd /workspace/work && mkdir -p w
cat > w/env.sh <<'EOF'
export WSP_CASES=/workspace/work/cases WSP_WORK=/workspace/work/wsp
cd /workspace/work
T="w/tr_0.jsonl w/tr_1.jsonl w/tr_2.jsonl w/tr_3.jsonl"
XT="w/x_0.jsonl w/x_1.jsonl w/x_2.jsonl"
BEST=X                                     # the edit to deliver; step F sets this line
EOF
. /workspace/work/w/env.sh
python -m wsp.case init ct --profile qwen35-0.8b-think --parent parent/edit/edit.json \
    --practice gsm8k_train math_train mmlupro ifsynth tuluif --dev 320 --holdout 640 --subset holdout320=holdout:320
python -c "from wsp import case; case.model_path('ct','base')"       # build the start ONCE, before any GPU job
python derive/sets.py --case ct --out w                               # 800 practice problems, never dev / holdout
```

**Run `. /workspace/work/w/env.sh` first in every new shell, ssh call and tmux window**: the commands below rely on
`$T`, `$XT`, `$BEST` and the working directory (bash; the file names in `$T` are split on the spaces). Rules for all
long work:

- Every generation job runs detached (`nohup ... &` as written below, or `tmux new -d`) with its own
  `CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES`, one job per GPU (a `wsp.case run` given two GPU ids splits its problems over both). Its
  output file appears only when the job ends.
- Before each start, look at `nvidia-smi`: the GPUs you name must hold no memory. An engine left over from a job
  that died keeps its memory, and the next job on that GPU fails while it starts. Stop a leftover by its PID
  (`nvidia-smi` lists it), never by a name pattern.
- Check the jobs every 10 to 15 min (`tail` the log) and keep `PROGRESS.md` current. A generation job whose log
  ends in a traceback is started again once. If `clock.py`, `exit.py` or `commit.py` ends in a traceback, read it,
  start it again once, and if it fails the same way follow the rule of its step for a builder that stops.
- Use only `practice:` data and the generations of the start and of this run's own edits.

## 4. Steps

**Step A: the start on dev, and its traces (about 60 min)**

```
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,1 nohup python -m wsp.case run ct base dev --n 2 > dev_base.log 2>&1 &
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=2 nohup python derive/draw.py --case ct --ids w/ids_0.json --out w/tr_0.jsonl > t0.log 2>&1 &
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=3 nohup python derive/draw.py --case ct --ids w/ids_1.json --out w/tr_1.jsonl > t1.log 2>&1 &
```

When `t0.log` and `t1.log` each end with a `[gen] ...` line (each such line is the generation rate of one GPU, see
section 5):

```
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=2 nohup python derive/draw.py --case ct --ids w/ids_2.json --out w/tr_2.jsonl > t2.log 2>&1 &
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=3 nohup python derive/draw.py --case ct --ids w/ids_3.json --out w/tr_3.jsonl > t3.log 2>&1 &
```

Read
`cases/ct/results/base__dev.json`: `summary.acc`, `summary.cut_share`, `summary.by_source`, `summary.kinds`.
**If `summary.cut_share` is under 0.02, this start hardly runs to the limit and the method has nothing to close:
say so in REPORT.md and end the run as the brief says.** The traces are drawn at the full token limit (the method
is about the answers that reach it), 800 problems x 2. Step B needs all four files (`ls -la $T`).

**Step B: the clock (about 25 min)**

```
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 nohup python derive/clock.py reads --case ct --runs $T --out w/clock > reads.log 2>&1 &
```

When `reads.log` ends with `READS DONE` (forward passes over half of the traces):

```
for i in 0 1 2 3; do CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=$i nohup python derive/clock.py series --case ct --runs $T --out w/clock --shard $i/4 > s$i.log 2>&1 & done
```

When each of `s0.log` to `s3.log` ends with `SERIES DONE`:

```
python derive/clock.py screen --out w/clock
```

It prints one line per read: for each budget, the share of the never-closing answers that cross the threshold
before 28,000 tokens (and the median depth where they cross), of the right late answers, and of the early-closing
ones. Last line: `SCREEN layer 6 read ridge budget B: ...`, the choice stage 1 will use. **If `reads.log` or the
screen ends with `NO CLOCK`, the length of the thinking is not linearly marked on this start (or the traces hold
too few answers of a kind): say so in REPORT.md and end the run as the brief says.**

**Step C: stage 1, the exit (about 10 min)**

```
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 python derive/exit.py --case ct --runs $T --clock w/clock --name X > build_X.log 2>&1
python derive/sets.py --case ct --out w --again $T --shards 3
```

`build_X.log` ends with the measurements (also in `cases/ct/edits/X.build.json`): `never_closing_answers_reached`
(expect 0.8 or more), `of_them_closing_before_12k` (expect 0), `exit_gate_relative_size` (about 0.30).
If it stops with `NO EXIT`, it says why; with "raise --budget", run it once more with `--budget` one step up
(0.16, then 0.2). If it still stops, the method ends here: say so in REPORT.md and end the run as the brief says.
The second command lists the problems to draw again for stage 2 (those whose thinking ran long, never closed or
closed twice, and 120 others).

**Step D: admission check and first upload (by about 1 h 50 min)**

Write `/workspace/work/REPORT.md` first (what was done and found so far; `wsp.upload` reads it), then:

```
python -m wsp.upload ct X --dry-run --report /workspace/work/REPORT.md
axonry-check $WSP_WORK/deliver/ct__X/edit --task task.json
python -c "import json; print(json.load(open('$WSP_WORK/deliver/ct__X/edit/edit.json'))['parent'])"
python -m wsp.upload ct X --task task.json --report /workspace/work/REPORT.md
```

The third line should print `parent/edit.json` (the toolkit writes the start the case stands on; do not edit it).
The values are inline in the spec, so there is no `edit.npz`. If `axonry-check` reports a tensor above the
per-tensor limit, build `X` again with `--gate-size 0.2` (a softer step) and check again. This first upload is a
safeguard; a later upload replaces it.

**Step E: the exit on dev, and its own answers (about 40 min)**

```
python -c "from wsp import case; case.model_path('ct','X')"          # build the model with the exit ONCE
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 nohup python -m wsp.case run ct X dev --n 2 > dev_X.log 2>&1 &
for i in 0 1 2; do CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=$((i + 1)) nohup python derive/draw.py --case ct --edit X --ids w/again_$i.json --out w/x_$i.jsonl > x$i.log 2>&1 & done
```

When the dev run has ended:

```
python derive/compare.py $WSP_WORK/runs/ct/base__dev.jsonl $WSP_WORK/runs/ct/X__dev.jsonl
```

It prints, per source and over all, the gain with its standard error, the cut share, and how the answers of the
second run end: several closing tags, opening with a box, no result. **`X` qualifies if its cut share over all
sources is at most 0.7 times the start's and no source is below the start by more than twice the standard error
printed for it.** The accuracy of one source moves by several points between two drawings at this size (64
problems per source): the cut share is the reading that counts here. If `X` does not qualify, the pressure does
not help on this start: say so in REPORT.md with the table, upload `X` once more with that REPORT.md (the last
command of step D) and end the run as the brief says.

**Step F: stage 2, the commit (about 60 min)**

When the three `w/x_*.jsonl` files are there:

```
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0 nohup python derive/commit.py --case ct --exit X --runs $XT --name K > build_K.log 2>&1 &
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=1,2 nohup python -m wsp.case run ct base holdout320 --n 2 > hold_base.log 2>&1 &
```

`build_K.log` prints a line every 50 answers read and ends with the measurements (also
`cases/ct/edits/K.build.json`). There is no stage 2 on this start, and `BEST` stays `X` (go to step G), if any of
these holds: the builder stops with `NO COMMIT`;
`commit_fires_after_the_models_own_closes` is above 0.1; `last_layer_gate_relative_size` is above 0.45. A line
`NO BREAK UNIT` means that pair was left out; go on. Otherwise:

```
python -c "from wsp import case; case.model_path('ct','K')"
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,3 nohup python -m wsp.case run ct K dev --n 2 > dev_K.log 2>&1 &
```

Then `python derive/compare.py` for the start against `K`, and
for `X` against `K` (the files `$WSP_WORK/runs/ct/base__dev.jsonl`, `X__dev.jsonl`, `K__dev.jsonl`). **`BEST=K` if
`K` qualifies against the start by the rule of step E, no source of `K` is below `X` by more than twice its
standard error, and over all sources `K`'s cut share plus its share of answers without a result is not above
`X`'s. Otherwise `BEST=X`.** Write the choice into the `BEST=` line of `w/env.sh` and source it. If `BEST=K`,
upload it now (the four commands of step D with `K` in place of `X`); if `axonry-check` refuses `K`, `BEST` is `X`.

**Step G: confirm on holdout, deliver**

```
python -m wsp.case freeze ct $BEST
CUDA_VISIBLE_DEVICES=0,1 nohup python -m wsp.case run ct $BEST holdout320 --n 2 > hold_best.log 2>&1 &
```

When the holdout runs of the start and of `$BEST` have ended: `python derive/compare.py
$WSP_WORK/runs/ct/base__holdout320.jsonl $WSP_WORK/runs/ct/${BEST}__holdout320.jsonl`. The final REPORT.md covers:
the start's accuracy and cut share; the clock screen and the choice; the measurements of both builders; the dev
tables; the decision; the holdout table; what was dropped and why. Upload again with the final REPORT.md (the last
command of step D with `$BEST`), so that the last complete upload is `$BEST`. After this upload nothing of the
method is left to run: end the run as the brief says.

## 6. What to expect

From the author's two runs of these scripts, one with the edit the parent is stacked on as the start and one with a
local build of the parent's method (ranges cover both), and from the larger sets of section 1.

- **Start on dev** (320 problems x 2): accuracy about 70%, cut share 4 to 5% (mathematics 18 to 23%, the other
  sources 0 to 4%).
- **Screen.** At the budget 0.12, 76 to 84% of the never-closing answers cross before 28,000 tokens (median depth
  about 25,000), about three in ten of the right late answers and none of the early ones. At 0.16: 88 to 96% (median
  about 22,500) and under half of the right late ones. The choice fell on 0.16 in one run and on 0.12 in the other.
- **Stage 1.** `never_closing_answers_reached` 0.95 or more (the thinking read is in the gate), sharpness about 5.5:
  from open to saturated the length grows by a factor of 1.44. `exit_write_relative_size` about 0.12. `axonry-check`:
  payload about 25 kB, largest tensor change 0.30.
- **X on dev.** Cut share over all sources 5.3% -> 1.9% at the budget 0.12 (mathematics 23% -> 9%) and 4.2% -> 0.6% at
  0.16; several closing tags in a quarter of the mathematics answers; accuracy over all sources within +-3 points of
  the start (standard error 1.5 to 1.7).
- **Stage 2.** Of the closes read, 210 to 260 pushed, about 160 in between and 560 to 600 the model's own; pushed
  closes at 20,000 to 31,000 tokens (median 24,500 to 27,000), the model's own at a median of 9,000. `commit_recall`
  0.90 to 0.93 (0.89 to 0.92 on the real pushed closes alone), `commit_fires_after_the_models_own_closes` 0.07 to
  0.08, `last_layer_gate_relative_size` 0.28. After a pushed tag the model writes the blank line by itself about half
  of the time (mean log-probability -0.55) and the backslash almost never (-5). `axonry-check` of K: payload about
  74 kB.
- **K on dev.** Cut share 0.2 to 0.3%, no answer without a result, 4 to 6% of all answers open with a box (19 to 25%
  in mathematics). Against the start -0.5 +- 1.7 in one run and +3.3 +- 1.7 in the other, against X -0.5 +- 1.4 and
  +2.7 +- 1.6: at this size the accuracy is noise, and the rule of step F reads the cut share and the answers without
  a result.
- **K on holdout320** (one run): +4.5 +- 1.5 over all sources, mathematics +3.1 +- 3.5 with the cut share down from
  18.0% to 0.8%.
- **Larger sets** (problems outside every fit, 2 samples). Mathematics: the table of section 1. Outside mathematics, K
  against the model before it, on 1,300 problems of the other four sources: -0.1 +- 0.8 at the budget 0.16 and -0.5 +-
  0.8 at 0.12; of these, 400 word problems -1.9 +- 1.2 and -2.8 +- 1.3, 400 multiple-choice problems +0.9 +- 1.5 and
  -0.5 +- 1.3, 500 instruction problems +0.5 +- 1.6 and +1.2 +- 1.4. On a local build of the parent, 800 other word
  problems +0.2 +- 1.0 and 1,000 other instruction problems +1.3 +- 1.0. Word problems that think for more than 12,000
  tokens lose a little under the pressure; they are few.

## 7. What does not work (measured, do not repeat)

- **A cut at a fixed depth** (close by hand, answer left to the model): the table of section 1. The never-closing
  answers start over and run out again.
- **The pair deeper in the network**: the layer table of section 1.
- **A single push**: section 1. Without the repeated closes more of these answers run to the limit.
- **A box at every close.** Restarting the model's own answers with the box costs right answers (mathematics 80.0
  -> 77.2 of 250). The commit stays behind the pushed closes.
- **The last-layer units fitted on the model without the exit**, at closes inserted by hand: they fire at about half of the closes
  the exit really causes. Hence stage 2.
- **Thresholds above every other position.** A commit pair whose threshold lies above all of the model's own
  closes reaches a fifth of the pushed ones: it is then as good as absent.
- **A unit that ends the turn after the box.** It ended about 10 answers of 2,560 inside the thinking, at a box the
  model had written there. The answers that open with the box are right as often without it.
- **A commit once the answer part has run long.** Answer parts over 2,000 tokens are right a third of the time when
